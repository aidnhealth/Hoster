Hoster

Your machine as a hosting platform — no cloud, no accounts, no monthly bill.

Point it at a project folder. It detects the stack, builds it, runs it, gives it a Postgres database, and puts it behind a URL. Front-end and back-end both. Works on your LAN with no internet at all.

Two commands

$ hoster deploy ./my-app
  http://my-app.hoster.localhost ← local URL, no DNS setup
$ hoster share my-app
  ← temporary public HTTPS tunnel when you need it

.localhost names resolve on this machine with zero DNS config. For other devices on your Wi‑Fi, use the LAN URL on each app card — or run sudo ./setup.sh for *.hoster.local. Full guide: docs/SETUP.md.

Auto-detects the stack

Dockerfile, Nixpacks (Node, Python, Go, …), or plain static files. React and friends build to assets with SPA fallback.

Postgres per app

Created on first deploy and injected as DATABASE_URL. Your app just reads it.

HTTPS on your LAN

Caddy terminates TLS with an internal CA. Share publicly only when you ask, via a Cloudflare quick tunnel.

Survives reboots

Apps come back when you log in. Dashboard and CLI — same power, whichever you prefer.
